## What Is Goal Setting?

For each reporting period (usually a term), pupils choose a goal for each of their academic subjects. A goal is the percentage that the pupil wants to achieve in that subject. Next to each goal, the pupil writes a strategy: what they will do to reach the goal. Teachers read the goals and strategies, and they can add a comment with advice. The goals then appear next to the pupil’s marks in the mark book, so that the pupil and their parents can see how the pupil is doing against each goal.

Not every school uses goal setting. A school may also use it for some grades only.

## For Pupils: Setting Your Goals

### When You Can Set Your Goals

Your school decides when you can set your goals. For each reporting period, the school opens a window of time for goal setting. You can enter and change your goals only while that window is open. After the window closes, you can still see your goals, but you cannot change them.

### Finding Goal Setting

1.  Log in to ADAM with **Pupil Login**.
2.  Under the **Academic** heading, click **Academic goals**.

![](assets/screenshots/goal-setting/goal-setting-06.png)

If you cannot see **Academic goals**, see [Help! I can’t see Academic goals!](#help-i-cant-see-academic-goals)

ADAM opens the **Academic Goal Setting** page. This page lists your goals in a table with the columns **Subject**, **Goal**, **Strategy** and **Actions**. The table is grouped by reporting period, with the most recent period first.

While the goal-setting window is open, a message at the top of the page starts “Goals and strategies for …”. It tells you the reporting period, the closing date and time, and how long you have left. Each subject in that reporting period also has an **edit** link in the **Actions** column.

### Setting Your Goals

1.  On the **Academic Goal Setting** page, click **edit** next to any subject. ADAM opens a form that lists all your subjects for that reporting period, so you can set every goal in one go.
2.  For each subject:
    -   Type your goal in the box next to the subject’s name. The goal is a percentage, as a whole number from 0 to 100.
    -   Under **My strategy to reach this goal:**, describe what you will do to reach the goal. The box shows an example strategy to get you started.
3.  Click **Save**. ADAM saves your goals and takes you back to the **Academic Goal Setting** page.

Next to most subjects, ADAM suggests a goal, for example: “Based on your past performance in English, ADAM suggests a goal of **75%**”. ADAM works out this suggestion from your recent results in that subject. It is only a suggestion: you choose your own goal. If ADAM has no recent results for a subject, it does not suggest a goal for that subject.

A good goal is one that you can reach if you work for it. A good strategy is specific. “I will do five extra Mathematics problems every evening” is a better strategy than “I will work harder”.

### Changing Your Goals

While the goal-setting window is open, you can change your goals as often as you like. Click **edit**, make your changes and click **Save**.

Your teachers may also be able to change your goals. If a goal needs to change after the window has closed, speak to your teacher.

> [!WARNING]
> Click **Save** before the window closes. If the window closes while you are filling in the form, ADAM does not save your goals. See [Help! ADAM says my goals were not saved!](#help-adam-says-my-goals-were-not-saved)

### Reading Your Teacher’s Comments

Your teachers can add a comment to each of your goals. On the **Academic Goal Setting** page, the comment appears in the **Strategy** column, below your strategy. It starts with **Teacher comment** and the teacher’s name.

Teachers can also write private notes about your goals for other staff members. You will not see these notes.

### Seeing Your Goals in the Mark Book

If your school lets you see the mark book, your goals also appear there:

1.  Under the **Academic** heading, click **Mark book**.
2.  Click the reporting period you want to see, for example **Term 1 Marks**.

Each subject has a **Goal** row that shows your goal percentage, your **Strategy:** and, if your teacher has commented, the **Teacher Comment:**. A small trophy icon next to an assessment mark shows that you reached your goal in that assessment. If you hold your mouse over the trophy, ADAM shows “You achieved your goal!”.

![](assets/screenshots/goal-setting/goal-setting-10.png)

If the page has a **Mark Book Summary** table at the top, that table also has a **Goal** column.

## For Parents: Seeing Your Child’s Goals

You see your child’s goals in the mark book:

1.  Log in to ADAM with **Parent Login**. If you have not logged in before, see [Logging on to ADAM: A Guide for Parents](logging-on-to-adam-a-guide-for-parents.md#logging-on-to-adam-a-guide-for-parents).
2.  On your child’s card, under the **Academic** heading, click **Mark book**.
3.  Click the reporting period you want to see, for example **Term 1 Marks**.

You will see the same goals, strategies, teacher comments and trophy icons that your child sees. These are described in [Seeing Your Goals in the Mark Book](#seeing-your-goals-in-the-mark-book). You will not see the private notes that teachers write for other staff members.

Goals appear only after your child has set them, and only if your school shows the mark book to parents.

### Can I Set or Change My Child’s Goals?

Usually not. Most schools let only pupils set goals, so that each goal belongs to the pupil and is agreed with their teachers.

If your school does allow parents to set goals, you will see **Academic goals** under the **Academic** heading on your child’s card. It works in the same way as described in [Setting Your Goals](#setting-your-goals). Talk to your child before you change anything: when you click **Save**, what you have entered replaces your child’s goals and strategies.

## Help! I Can’t See Academic Goals!

**Academic goals** appears only if your school has turned on goal setting for you. Many schools use goal setting for some grades only. If you think you should be able to set goals, ask your teacher.

Parents normally do not see **Academic goals**. Parents see their child’s goals in the mark book instead: see [For Parents: Seeing Your Child’s Goals](#for-parents-seeing-your-childs-goals).

## Help! There Is No Edit Link!

The **edit** link appears only while the goal-setting window is open. If there is no **edit** link, the window has not opened yet or it has already closed. The same applies if ADAM shows “No goals have been set.” with no **edit** link next to it.

Ask your teacher when goal setting opens. If the window has closed and a goal needs to change, speak to your teacher.

## Help! ADAM Says My Goals Were Not Saved!

If you see the message “Goal setting for Term 1 has closed, so those goals were not saved.”, the goal-setting window closed between opening the form and clicking **Save**. ADAM did not keep the changes you made. Speak to your teacher, who may be able to enter your goals for you.

## Help! One of My Subjects Is Missing!

The form lists the academic subjects that you are registered for in that reporting period. If a subject is missing, ask your teacher. The school may need to check which classes you are registered in.

## Help! I Can’t See the Goals in the Mark Book!

Goals appear in the mark book only after they have been set, and only for the reporting period in which they were set. Check that you have chosen the right reporting period. If **Mark book** is not shown under the **Academic** heading, your school has not made the mark book available to you. Contact the school if you think you should be able to see it.
