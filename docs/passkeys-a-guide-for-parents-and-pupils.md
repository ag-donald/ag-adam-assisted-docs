# Passkeys: A Guide for Parents and Pupils

**A passkey lets you log in to ADAM with your fingerprint, your face or your device’s screen-lock PIN, instead of typing your ID number or username and your password. This guide explains how to set one up and how to look after it.**

## What Is a Passkey?

A passkey is a small piece of information that lives on your phone, tablet or computer. When you log in, your device asks you to confirm that it is you, using the same fingerprint, face recognition or PIN that you use to unlock it. Your device then tells ADAM “yes, this is the right person”.

ADAM never sees your fingerprint or your face. They do not leave your device.

A passkey is an extra way to log in. It does not replace your password: your ID number (or username) and password still work, and you will need them on any device where you have not set up a passkey.

## What You Need

You need one of the following:

-   A phone or tablet with fingerprint or face unlock, such as an iPhone with Face ID or Touch ID, or an Android phone with a fingerprint sensor.
-   A laptop or desktop computer with a fingerprint reader, Windows Hello or Touch ID.
-   A hardware security key, such as a YubiKey.

You do not need to install an app. Passkeys are built into up-to-date versions of iOS, Android, Windows, macOS and all the main web browsers (Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox).

> [!NOTE]
> Set up passkeys only on devices that you alone use. Anyone who can unlock a device that holds your passkey can also log in to your ADAM account. On a shared or public computer, log in with your password instead.

## Setting Up a Passkey

1.  Log in to ADAM as usual. Parents use **Parent Login** with their ID or passport number and password; pupils use **Pupil Login** with their username and password. See [Logging on to ADAM: A Guide for Parents](logging-on-to-adam-a-guide-for-parents.md#logging-on-to-adam-a-guide-for-parents) if you have not logged in before.
2.  At the top of your portal page, you will see a card titled “**Log in with your fingerprint or face**”. Click the **Set up a passkey** button on that card.

    If you do not see the card, use the portal menu instead: under the **Security** heading, click **Manage your passkeys**.

3.  The **Passkeys** page opens. Click **Register a new passkey**.
4.  Your device asks you to confirm that it is you, with your fingerprint, your face or your PIN. On some devices it first asks where to save the passkey, for example in your phone’s password manager.
5.  The page reloads and your new passkey is listed.

ADAM names the new passkey after the place it was saved, for example “Windows Hello” or “Google Password Manager”. If ADAM does not recognise it, the name is “Passkey registered on” followed by the date. You can change the name at any time: see [Managing your passkeys](#managing-your-passkeys).

The “**Log in with your fingerprint or face**” card appears every time you log in with your password. It does not appear when you log in with a passkey. So you may still see it on a device where you have not set up a passkey yet, such as a second phone or a family computer. If you do not want a passkey on that device, you can ignore the card.

You can set up more than one passkey: one on each device that you use for ADAM. If you lose one device, the others continue to work.

## Logging in with a Passkey

1.  Open the ADAM log-in page on a device where you have set up a passkey: **Parent Login** for parents, **Pupil Login** for pupils.
2.  Click **Log in with a passkey**.
3.  Your device asks you to confirm with your fingerprint, your face or your PIN.
4.  ADAM logs you in and shows your portal page.

You do not type an ID number, username or password. On a phone, this usually takes a few seconds.

On a desktop computer without a fingerprint reader, your browser may offer to use your phone instead. It shows a QR code: scan it with your phone, and confirm on the phone.

## Managing Your Passkeys

To see your passkeys, log in and, under the **Security** heading in the portal menu, click **Manage your passkeys**. The **Passkeys** page lists each passkey with its **Name**, the date it was **Created** and the date it was **Last used**.

-   To change a passkey’s name, click **rename** next to it, type the new name (for example, “My phone”) and click **Rename**. The name only helps you to tell your passkeys apart. It does not change the passkey.
-   To remove a passkey, click **remove** next to it. ADAM asks “**Remove passkey?**” and warns that the passkey can no longer be used to sign in. Click **Remove** to confirm.

Remove a passkey when you have lost, sold or given away the device that holds it, or when you no longer use that device for ADAM.

Removing a passkey from ADAM does not delete it from your device, and deleting it from your device (for example, by resetting your phone) does not remove it from ADAM. When you replace a device, remove the old passkey here and set up a new one on the new device.

To return to your portal page, click **Back** at the top of the **Passkeys** page.

## Help! I’ve Lost My Phone!

Your account is still protected. The passkey is locked behind your phone’s screen lock, so a person who finds the phone cannot use it without your fingerprint, face or PIN.

On another device, log in with your ID number (or username) and password. Open **Manage your passkeys** and remove the passkey for the lost phone.

If you cannot remember your password, parents can set a new one with the **New account? Forgotten password?** button on the **Parent Login** page: see [Help! I’ve forgotten my password!](logging-on-to-adam-a-guide-for-parents.md#help-ive-forgotten-my-password). Pupils should ask the school to help them reset their password.

The school cannot remove a parent’s or pupil’s passkey for you. Because your password always works, you can always log in and remove it yourself.

## Help! I’ve Got a New Phone!

If your new phone uses the same Apple, Google or Microsoft account as your old phone, your passkeys may move across by themselves. This depends on your account settings, not on ADAM.

If they do not, treat the new phone as a new device. Log in with your password on the new phone, set up a new passkey, and then remove the old phone’s passkey from the **Passkeys** page.

## Help! I Can’t See the Passkey Button!

ADAM shows the **Log in with a passkey** button only when your browser supports passkeys. If you cannot see it:

-   Check that your browser is up to date.
-   Check that you are not in private or incognito browsing mode.
-   If you opened ADAM from a link inside another app (a messaging app or the school’s app, for example), the link may have opened inside that app instead of in your phone’s browser. These built-in browsers do not support passkeys. Open ADAM in your phone’s own browser, such as Safari or Chrome.

Until then, log in with your ID number (or username) and password as usual.

## Help! Setting Up the Passkey Failed!

If ADAM shows **Registration failed**, read the message under it. The most common cause is that the confirmation on your device was cancelled or took too long. Click **Register a new passkey** and try again, and confirm with your fingerprint, face or PIN when your device asks.

The private-browsing and in-app-browser problems described in [Help! I can’t see the passkey button!](#help-i-cant-see-the-passkey-button) can also stop a passkey being saved. On those browsers, the **Manage your passkeys** option still appears in the menu, but setting up a passkey will not work.

If it still fails, log in with your password as usual and contact the school.
