# Online Agreements

ADAM has the ability to ask staff, families and pupils to agree to, or decline, online agreements. These may include terms of enrolment, important notifications and so on.

## Creating Online Agreements

Online Agreements can be created for one of these audiences:

-   Families
-   Families (by Pupil Grade)
-   Families (by Pupil Subject)
-   Families (by Pupil Class)
-   Families (by Privilege Group)
-   Families (by Registration Status)
-   Pupils
-   Pupils (by Grade)
-   Pupils (by Privilege Group)
-   Pupils (by Registration Status)
-   Staff
-   Staff (by Privilege Group)
-   Applicants

Agreements can be drafted and saved via **Administration → Online Agreements → Manage Online Agreements**.

Setting up a new agreement is as simple as clicking on the “Add new Online Agreement” button.

When setting up a new agreement, the following screen is displayed:

![](assets/screenshots/online-agreements/online-agreements-01.png)

The agreement should be given a **name**. This is used to identify the broad contents and purpose of the document.

The **audience** can then be chosen. Parents, pupils and staff are prompted to agree to the terms the next time they log in to ADAM. The **Families** audiences are answered by parents, the **Pupils** audiences by pupils and the **Staff** audiences by staff.

An audience with “by” in its name is narrowed to part of that group. When one is chosen, ADAM shows a further field for the selection: **Grades**, **Subjects**, **Classes**, **Pupil Privilege Groups**, **Staff Privilege Groups** or **Registration Statuses**. Select the entries the agreement applies to. For example, an agreement for **Families (by Pupil Grade)** with Grade 8 selected is shown to the families of Grade 8 pupils.

The **Applicants** audience is a special case: applicants do not log in to ADAM, so they are shown any outstanding agreements as a step *during* the [online application](online-applications.md#responding-to-online-agreements) — after they have clicked **Start Application** and before they reach the application form.

The next two fields determine whether acceptance is required and which responses are acceptable. If “**Acceptance Required**” is set to “Yes”, then ADAM provides a single button for the user to click on which reads “I agree to the conditions stated above.” If this option is set to “No”, then an additional button is shown which reads “I DO NOT agree to the conditions stated above.”

However, should the second option be set to “No”, ADAM will also consider the possible responses listed in the next box: “**Acceptable Responses**”. These should be listed one-per-line. There can be as many as you’d like. Note that the first option will appear as a bright blue button (the “default” option) and any others will appear as a light blue button. The first option, therefore, should be the one that you expect most of your audience to choose.

**Agreement is Rescindable** sets whether a parent may withdraw their family’s response later and respond again. The default is “No”. Parents see the **rescind response** link only when this is set to “Yes”. See [Rescission of Online Agreements](#rescission-of-online-agreements) below.

We can now set, with **Email Responses**, whether we would like the text of the agreement and the response chosen by the parent to be **emailed** to them.

> [!WARNING]
> Note that changing this setting on old agreements will cause users to be emailed a copy, even if they responded a long time ago.

> [!WARNING]
> If one parent agrees to the conditions, both parents will be emailed a copy of the agreement and the response. This does not hold for divorced or otherwise split families where each family member must agree separately.

Next, we have the **text of the agreement**. This can be formatted using some basic formatting options as shown in the toolbar above the text entry area.

At the bottom of the screen is an option to **change the ordering of the agreements**. If a user has more than one agreement to view, they will be shown in the order specified.

## Other Options

From the list of agreements, each agreement will show the options to be edited or to view the associated report with the responses.

![](assets/screenshots/online-agreements/online-agreements-04.png)

The “**up**” and “**down**” options will simply change the list ordering of the agreements. Where there are long lists, it is easier to edit the agreement and, at the bottom, choose a new list position for the agreement.

### Editing Agreements

It is possible to edit an agreement by clicking on the **edit** link next to the agreement name.

If you edit the text of an agreement in any way, ADAM will automatically archive the old version of the agreement and create a new version thereof. The old version of the email will be archived and a new version created. The “version” that appears in the list of agreements will also be incremented to help avoid confusion.

**Users who agreed to the old version will be required to agree to the new version.** For this reason, cosmetic changes to the agreement should be kept to a minimum because any changes to the agreement, even cosmetic, will be interpreted by ADAM as a new agreement.

> [!WARNING]
> Above, we mentioned that changing the email setting on old agreements would cause the agreements to be emailed out. This only holds if the text of the agreement is not changed when the setting is turned on. If the text is changed, then the instruction to email the agreements is saved with the **new** agreement and not the old one.

### Disabling and Enabling Agreements

It is possible to disable an agreement. Disabling an agreement means that users will not be asked to agree.

> [!WARNING]
> Please be careful when enabling agreements, particularly ones that are not the latest version. Editing these older agreements will cause confusion in the numbering.

### Viewing Agreement Responses

Next to the appropriate agreement, click on the “report” option. Currently ADAM shows a simple list of the names of people who have responded to the agreement.

The report shows those who have responded as well as their response and, in a second list, those who have viewed the agreement but who have not yet responded:

![](assets/screenshots/online-agreements/online-agreements-07.png)

## Online Agreements in the Parent Portal

Parents and pupils are asked to respond to their outstanding agreements when they log in to the portal. ADAM sends them to the first outstanding agreement every time they open the main portal page, and they cannot see the portal until they have responded to every one. Parents are shown agreements for the **Families** audiences, and pupils are shown agreements for the **Pupils** audiences. If a password change is required, ADAM asks for it first.

ADAM records one response per family. Once either parent on a family profile has responded, the other parent is not asked again.

Where **Acceptance Required** is set to “Yes”, the only button is “**I agree to the conditions stated above.**”, so a parent or pupil cannot use the portal without accepting the agreement.

Parents can view the agreements their family has responded to under the **Family** heading of the portal, by clicking **Manage Online Agreements**. Pupils have no equivalent page, so they cannot view or change their responses after answering.

For step-by-step instructions that you can send to parents and pupils, see [Online Agreements: A Guide for Parents and Pupils](online-agreements-a-guide-for-parents-and-pupils.md#online-agreements-a-guide-for-parents-and-pupils).

### Rescission of Online Agreements

A parent can rescind (withdraw) their family’s response only if the agreement has **Agreement is Rescindable** set to “Yes”. The default is “No”. Where it is “Yes”, a **rescind response** link appears next to the agreement on the parent’s **Manage Online Agreements** page. Pupils cannot rescind their responses.

When a parent rescinds a response:

-   The original response is kept, with the date and time it was given and the date and time it was rescinded. Neither the parent nor the school can modify it.
-   The rescinded response moves to the parent’s **Past Agreements** list.
-   ADAM treats the agreement as outstanding again, and the family must respond to it before they can use the portal. If **Acceptance Required** is “Yes”, they can only agree again.
-   Because there is one response per family, rescinding clears the response for both parents on the family profile.

The parent-facing steps are in [Online Agreements: A Guide for Parents and Pupils](online-agreements-a-guide-for-parents-and-pupils.md#changing-your-response).

### Viewing Rescinded Agreements

When viewing the [Online Agreement report](#viewing-agreement-responses), ADAM will list all rescissions in that report, including the date the agreement was initially responded to and the date and time that the agreement was rescinded.
